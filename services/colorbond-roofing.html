What this service covers

Roofing Brisbane designs and installs Colorbond metal roofing for SEQ homes that need heat reflection, storm performance and a clean coastal look. We work with common Australian profiles and colour ranges suited to Brisbane humidity and Sunshine Coast salt air.

Who typically needs this

  • New builds specifying metal roofing
  • Owners replacing tired tile or old metal sheets
  • Renovations matching cladding to roof colour
  • Light commercial canopies and carports

Common examples we handle in SEQ

  • Corrugated and trapezoidal Colorbond on single-storey brick homes (Brisbane suburbs)
  • Coastal homes needing corrosion-aware screws and flashings
  • Re-roof over existing frames where structure allows
  • Colour-matched barge, ridge and valley flashings

How the process works

  • Site measure and substrate check
  • Material selection (profile, colour, insulation/sisalation)
  • Strip or prepare deck, set falls and flashings
  • Install sheets, ridges, barges and seal penetrations
  • Clean-down, magnet sweep and handover photos

Indicative cost ranges (Australia / SEQ)

Final price depends on access, pitch, storeys, material colour and storm damage. Ranges below are for orientation only — we confirm on-site.

  • Small carport / patio metal roof: often mid hundreds to low thousands
  • Standard single-storey house re-sheet: typically mid-to-high thousands depending on m²
  • Complex multi-level or custom flashings: quoted individually
