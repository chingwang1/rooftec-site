What this service covers

In SEQ storms, undersized or poorly fallen gutters overflow into eaves and ceilings. Rooftec installs and repairs quad, fascia and custom box gutters that move water off the building fast.

Who typically needs this

  • Overflow during heavy rain
  • Rotting fascia or stained eaves
  • Sagging or leaking box gutters behind parapets
  • Leaf and debris issues on tree-lined blocks

Common examples we handle in SEQ

  • Full gutter replacements colour-matched to Colorbond roofs
  • Custom box gutters with correct falls and outlets
  • Downpipe upgrades and spreader fixes
  • Valley iron repairs tied into metal roofing

How the process works

  • Inspect fall, outlets and overflow paths
  • Design capacity for local storm intensity
  • Fabricate / supply and install
  • Seal junctions to roof and cladding
  • Test with hose where practical
