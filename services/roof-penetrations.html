What this service covers

Every hole in a metal roof is a future leak if flashings are rushed. Roofing Brisbane details penetrations for solar, skylights, flues and services so water sheds around the object — not under the sheet.

Who typically needs this

  • New solar array penetrations
  • Skylight installs or replacements
  • Heater flues and kitchen exhausts
  • Leaking collars on existing services

Common examples we handle in SEQ

  • Dektite / EPDM collars sized to pipe OD
  • Soaker flashings for larger kerbs
  • Re-flashing after DIY solar installs
  • Combining penetrations with sheet replacement

How the process works

  • Locate rafters and sheet laps
  • Cut and reinforce opening as required
  • Install proprietary or custom flashings
  • Seal and water-test where possible

FAQs — Roof Penetrations & Flashings

Who cuts the roof for solar?

We can prepare and flash roofs for solar electricians, or re-flash after a poorly sealed install.

Will sealant alone fix a penetration leak?

Rarely long-term. Correct mechanical flashing is the durable fix; sealant is only a helper.
