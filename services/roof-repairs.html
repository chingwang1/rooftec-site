What this service covers

Most SEQ roof calls start with a leak after a storm or a ceiling stain that keeps returning. Roofing Brisbane focuses on metal roof diagnostics — finding the path of water, not just sealing the first wet spot.

Who typically needs this

  • Active ceiling leaks after rain
  • Sheets lifting in high wind
  • Rusted screws, loose flashings or failed sealant
  • Insurance storm damage assessments

Common examples we handle in SEQ

  • Valley and apron flashing leaks on older metal roofs
  • Back-pitched box gutter overflow into eaves
  • Skylight and solar penetration seepage
  • Scattered sheet replacement after hail or branch impact

How the process works

  • Inspect roof, ceiling and external drainage
  • Photograph defects and explain cause in plain English
  • Temporary make-safe if needed
  • Permanent repair: sheets, screws, flashings or sealant systems as appropriate
  • Optional maintenance plan for ageing roofs
