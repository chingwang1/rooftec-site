What this service covers

When repairs stack up or tiles/metal are at end of life, a full replacement is often cheaper than another decade of call-outs. We re-roof SEQ homes with modern Colorbond systems sized for heat and summer storms.

Who typically needs this

  • Multiple active leak points each wet season
  • Widespread rust, brittle tiles or failed sarking
  • Selling or renovating and need a long-life roof
  • Insurance total loss / major storm rebuilds

Common examples we handle in SEQ

  • Tile-to-Colorbond conversions on suitable structures
  • Like-for-like metal re-sheet with upgraded flashings
  • Adding insulation blankets / thermal breaks during re-roof
  • Re-pitch or drainage upgrades where water pools

How the process works

  • Structural and substrate assessment
  • Quote with m², profile, colour and inclusions list
  • Protect gardens and schedule skip/recycling
  • Strip, dispose, install underlay/sheets/flashings
  • Final clean, magnet sweep, walkthrough

Indicative cost ranges (Australia / SEQ)

Final price depends on access, pitch, storeys, material colour and storm damage. Ranges below are for orientation only — we confirm on-site.

  • Small dwellings / granny flats: often lower-to-mid five figures depending on m²
  • Average suburban houses: project-based quotes after measure
  • Heritage, steep pitch or multi-wing homes: custom staging

FAQs — Roof Replacement Specialists — SEQ

How long does a house re-roof take?

Many single-storey metal re-sheets complete in a few days of good weather once materials are on site. Multi-level or complex roofs take longer.

Will my solar stay?

Panels usually need temporary removal. We coordinate with licensed solar electricians so arrays are refitted cleanly.

Do you remove old roofs?

Yes — strip-out and disposal are included in full replacement scopes unless you arrange recycling yourself.
