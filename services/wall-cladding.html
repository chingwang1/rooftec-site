What this service covers

Metal wall cladding is popular on Queensland renovations and new builds for low maintenance and a sharp coastal aesthetic. We install Colorbond cladding systems with flashings that keep wind-driven rain out.

Who typically needs this

  • Extension or garage façade upgrades
  • Feature walls matching a new metal roof
  • Weatherboard replacement with metal
  • Light commercial shopfronts and sheds

Common examples we handle in SEQ

  • Horizontal and vertical cladding profiles
  • Gable infills colour-matched to roof
  • Coastal installs with appropriate fixings
  • Integration with windows, corners and base details

How the process works

  • Measure elevations and openings
  • Select profile, colour and vapour/insulation layers
  • Install battens/sheets and flash all junctions
  • Seal and finish trims

Is metal cladding noisy in rain?

With correct install and building wrap, rain noise is usually modest. We can discuss acoustic options for bedrooms on weather walls.
